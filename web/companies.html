For companies

Preflight is an early prototype. This page says plainly what works today and what does not exist yet.

The path

  1. Try it in your browser. Open the demo on synthetic invoices, then add one of your own CSV exports. Nothing is uploaded. Read the pilot guide for how to export and what to check.
  2. Run a small trial. Check a few months of invoices, have a person review each finding, and note which ones were real. A small contractor or a larger team can do this alone, with no setup.
  3. Tell us what you found. Use the feedback form. The counts-only pilot summary is a safe way to share results.

Roadmap, not available today

None of the following exists yet. Do not rely on it.

  • Single sign-on and user accounts
  • An audit trail kept on a server
  • A connection to an ERP or accounting system
  • An independent security review or compliance certification

Today the app is a static page. It has no server, no accounts and no integrations, and its findings are candidates for a person to review.

Before any real adoption

Treat Preflight as a second pair of eyes, not a control. It never pays, blocks or approves anything. See the terms and the privacy page.
