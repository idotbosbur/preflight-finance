Privacy

Preflight runs in your browser. Your invoice files are read on your device and are never uploaded.

What is stored, and where

  • In "your data" mode, the invoices you import and your review decisions are saved in this browser's IndexedDB. They stay on this device, in this browser profile.
  • The demo also keeps your review decisions on the synthetic invoices in IndexedDB.
  • Small preferences use browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage): whether the guided tour is on, whether the intro was shown, and which CSV columns you matched to which fields.

What is not collected

  • No uploads. Your CSV is parsed by code running in your browser.
  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • Visit counts: the site uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies. It records the page path, referring site, country, browser and device type, and identifies a visit only by a short-lived hash that Vercel discards after 24 hours. The part of app addresses after # is removed before anything is sent, and invoice files, rows and findings are never sent.
  • No advertising, no cookies set by the app, and no other tracking. Apart from page views, the app's only network requests are for its own files, the AI model described below, and the feedback form when you choose to send it.
  • AI model: the first time you match a bank or card statement, or add bills with item lines, your browser downloads a small public text model (all-MiniLM-L6-v2, about 23 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and keeps it in its cache. The model then runs on your device to compare payee names with supplier names, and item names with each other. Only the model files are downloaded; no invoice, statement or finding is sent to Hugging Face or anyone else. If the download fails, matching uses name words only.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel, which serves the app's files. Like most hosts, Vercel may keep standard server logs (for example IP address, time and page requested) when your browser loads those files. Your invoice data is not part of any request, because it never leaves your browser.

Deleting your data

  • Open Your data and choose "Delete my data". It erases your imported files, rows, findings, decisions and remembered column choices from this browser. The demo is separate and is not affected.
  • Or clear this site's data in your browser settings. That removes everything, including the small preferences above.

Because nothing is stored on a server, there is no copy for us to delete, and your data cannot be recovered if you delete it or your browser clears it.

Feedback

If you send the feedback form, your answers (and your email, if you give one) go to the site owner's Google Form, and Google's privacy policy applies to them. Nothing else from the app is sent, and the form never includes invoice data.

Contact

Send feedback through the feedback form. This prototype has no other contact channel yet.
