Terms of use

Preflight is a prototype provided as-is, to try out an idea.

What it is

A review aid. It looks through an invoice CSV for possible duplicate invoices and total mismatches, and shows them as candidates for a person to check. It runs in your browser.

What it is not

  • It is not accounting, financial, tax or legal advice.
  • It never pays, blocks, holds or approves an invoice, and it does not connect to your accounting or payment systems.
  • A finding is not a conclusion. It can be wrong, and it can miss things. You decide what to do, and you are responsible for your payments and records.
